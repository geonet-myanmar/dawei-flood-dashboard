Situation map · Tanintharyi Region, Myanmar · 26–27 September 2026

Floods and landslides in Dawei District

Record rain on 26–27 September flooded Dawei and Thayetchaung and brought hillsides down across Launglon, where people are dead, dozens are missing and a landslide on the only mapped road south stopped rescuers. Every place named in the Dawei Watch report is mapped here on MIMU geography.
